# Privacy Policy

Studio Klypi is committed to protecting your privacy.  
This policy outlines the data we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

## 1. Data Collected

When using the contact form, we collect:
- First name
- Email address

## 2. Purpose of Collection

Your data is used solely to respond to your message.

## 3. Data Retention

Personal data is stored for up to 12 months, unless deletion is requested.

## 4. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, delete, or oppose the use of your data.  
To exercise these rights: contact@studio-klypi.com

## 5. Cookies

No cookies are currently used for analytics or tracking purposes. This may change in the future and the policy will be updated accordingly.

## 6. Data Security

Your data is processed securely and confidentially.
